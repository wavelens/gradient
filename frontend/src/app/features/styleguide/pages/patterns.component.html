Settings Navigation

Settings pages for a project, task, cache, or team share one side navigation in the settings-navigation slot of gr-page-layout. Breadcrumbs end with "Settings". Page titles show the page name. Links the viewer can't use don't appear. Lists with only one link render nothing. Under 768px, the list is a scrolling row.
